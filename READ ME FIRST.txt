SELECT & PLAY - jukebox for Windows

To install:
 1. Double-click "Install Select and Play".
 2. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
 3. Leave the black window open until it says DONE. The jukebox then opens.

After that it waits in the tray (the icons by the clock). Click its icon to open it.
